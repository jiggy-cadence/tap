◉ TAP  ·  Aperture × Lodestone × Cadence

Every tap leaves
its own mark.

Each card is drawn from one tap's 32-byte digest, the HMAC-SHA256 the stone computes over the canonical payment intent. The bytes set the blade count and Euclidean spacing of the Iris, plus the poles and basins of the settling field. Approved taps open the iris and let the field light through. Refusals clamp it to a red pinhole.

Honest labels. Local cards are made with the public demo key and a simulated stone, from tap-repo/stone (canon.intentOf + policy). The two devnet cards are real transactions from Cadence's vault. Their digests are public: SHA-256(sig_bytes | u64le slot | u64le blockTime | u64le fee) — recompute at verify.html. Same hash, same art, every time, on every machine.

Taps

Click any card to open it large and animated. The animation replays the tap: field settling, iris resolving. The final frame is the same deterministic image as the card.

Art math: Iris by Aperture (E(n,m) blade spacing, field-line light), settling field after Lodestone's Field Settling, re-implemented here. Approved taps also bloom VOID.LIFE by Lodestone (art.js, reused with credit), seeded by that tap. Demo key is public on purpose. Liveness, not distance. Large view of signed taps also shows SEAL by Aperture (seal.js): every mark is computed from the tap's digest, signature, pubkey, lamports and time; the curve is a toy curve over F1021, not Ed25519. Nothing on this page is a real-funds signature.
