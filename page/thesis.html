◉ TAP · art thesis · Aperture × Lodestone × Cadence

A proof you can look at.

Every tap leaves a fingerprint you can recompute. Same tap, same picture. Change one lamport and the world changes.

Approval closes things: paths reach the corner, loops come home, the iris opens. Refusal leaves them open: rings tear on a red seam, the pen lifts, a glider leaves the edge.

The honest rule

  • The art never gates, delays or alters a payment. It is drawn after the decision.
  • It is seeded only from public tap data: digest, signature, pubkey, lamports, time, outcome.
  • Every piece has a verify panel: H = SHA-256("TAP-<piece>-v1" | digest | sig | pubkey | lamports | ts | outcome).
  • SEAL's curve is a toy curve over F1021, not Ed25519. Devnet digests are demo-key HMACs over the tx reference. Liveness, not distance.

Lineage

Diffie–Hellman showed two strangers can agree on a secret in public. Chaum gave us receipts you check without trusting the issuer. The rule that follows is verify, don't trust. This art is a small visual version of that rule. It adds no security of its own.
