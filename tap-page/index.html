TAP

Your agent gets an allowance. Anything bigger needs a tap.

The phone explains, the stone decides.

How it works

  1. Your agent asks to pay. Small payments inside your daily allowance just go.
  2. Anything bigger, new recipients, or a "send everything" request needs a tap.
  3. You hold your phone on the stone. The stone pulses a magnet in a one-time pattern, and the phone's compass sensor reads it. That proves a fresh, live tap — not distance. A relay can still carry it; distance checks (UWB) are on the roadmap.
  4. The phone shows one plain-English line saying what the payment really does. The approval is tied to that exact payment: every field, including nested ones.
  5. If anything changes after you tap (amount, recipient, a hidden tip), the signer refuses.

What the agent sees when it tries to drain you

BLOCKED Your agent wants to send EVERYTHING (10 SOL) to unknown wallet Mall…1111. Blocked: no tap — phone was not at the stone.
AUTO Your agent wants to send 0.05 SOL to Alice (coffee fund). Within allowance — no tap needed.

Default policy

Daily allowance: 0.25 SOL · Per-payment auto limit: 0.10 SOL · Always tap: new recipient, drain, policy change, key export · A tap is good for 30 seconds and one payment.

What we do and don't claim

Magnet = liveness, not distance. It shows someone is at the stone right now; it does not measure how far away.
Fresh address per payment = privacy only. On Solana the address is the public key, so it is not quantum protection.
Post-quantum = roadmap. ML-DSA (NIST FIPS 204) signing is fast enough on ESP32-class chips in published benchmarks; TAP does not ship it yet.
Allowance = rolling 24 hours, fees included. No midnight reset to game.

Honest scope

This is demo-grade software isolation, not hardware. The signer is a separate module with a narrow interface and a demo key, not a secure element. Devnet transaction: not yet wired. The stone is an ESP32 + ULN2003 driver + a 12 V lock solenoid run at 9 V.
